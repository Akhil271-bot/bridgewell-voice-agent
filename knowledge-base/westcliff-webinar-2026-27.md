# Westcliff University — Student Webinar Deck, 2026–27
Source: Westcliff International Office student webinar slides (2026–2027), added September 2026.
Use these figures as published by the university. Tuition is indicative and subject to change.

INTERNAL NOTE — never tell a student that the university has several intakes or starts per year.
Talk about one intake only: the one they named, or the next one.

## Campuses and accreditation
- Main campus: Irvine, California.
- Additional campuses: Los Angeles, San Francisco, Miami, Orlando, Dallas, Toronto (Canada).
- Approved by the California BPPE since 1993. Accreditations shown: WASC Senior College and University Commission, ACBSP, CHEA-recognised.

## Fast facts
- 9,079 students enrolled for Spring 2026; 103 nationalities; 10:1 student-faculty ratio (18:1 national average).
- 89% job placement after graduation; 94% student satisfaction; #2 ranked largest MBA programme in Southern California.
- These are university-reported figures, not guarantees.

## Programme formats
- Online remote: all programmes available 100% live online. NOT F-1 supported.
- Weekly: each course meets on campus once a week, 3 hours.
- Professional: on-campus classes one weekend (Friday to Sunday) per semester, plus online classwork. Some US states do not allow the professional format — the list must be confirmed with the International Office.
- Homework in all formats: discussion posts due Thursdays 11:59pm Pacific, peer responses Sundays 11:59pm, papers in APA format, projects often group work.

## CPT and OPT
- CPT full time (40 hrs): limited to 1 year to stay eligible for OPT. Part time (20 hrs): no duration limit.
- Bachelor's, master's and doctorate are eligible; undergraduates must complete one academic year first.
- Students on CPT register for an additional 1-credit course each semester.
- OPT: 1 year after graduation if eligible; STEM students can apply for 2 more years (3 total); once per degree level; full or part time.
- Never assess an individual student's eligibility or visa chances — that goes to Akhil.

## Career services

## Admission requirements
- Diploma and transcripts: minimum 2.0 GPA undergraduate, 2.5 graduate. A 3-year bachelor's degree is accepted.
- English proficiency: exam scores, MOI letter, or the REAL Pathway programme.
- No GMAT, GRE or SAT required.

| Programme | TOEFL | IELTS | Duolingo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bachelor's | 60 | 5.5 | 95 |

These are general requirements; specific programmes may differ.

## Scholarships and grants
- Automatic merit scholarship: awarded on application to incoming F-1 students with a minimum 3.0 GPA. $1,000–$5,000 a year depending on GPA.
- Other merit scholarships require a separate application. Total awards up to $10,000 a year.
- What a specific student would receive is not published — that goes to Akhil.

## Applications
Students must NOT apply on the Westcliff website. Applications go through their education consultant, who gives instructions for submitting.

## Why students choose Westcliff
Affordable tuition with per-semester payment plans and scholarships for international students; Irvine is ranked the #1 safest city in America (FBI crime statistics) and #1 best place to live in California (Time); multiple campuses; the REAL Communications Pathway for students without sufficient English, with concurrent degree enrollment; practical hands-on curriculum with CPT work off campus during the programme.
