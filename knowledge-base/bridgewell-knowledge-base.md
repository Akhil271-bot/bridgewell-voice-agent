**How to use these facts.** Every figure here is as the institution published it. Tuition is indicative, set by the university, and subject to change. Where a fact is not in this document, it is not known — say "Akhil will confirm that for you" rather than estimating.

# PART 1 — QUICK ANSWERS

## The single most important question on any Westcliff call

Does the student want to study **in the USA on an F-1 student visa**, or **from where they are**?

- **Live online** — every Westcliff programme is available 100% live online. **Live online is NOT F-1 supported.** A student who needs a US student visa cannot use this route.
- **On campus** — requires F-1 and admission. Tuition is higher, plus roughly $20,000 a year in living costs.

Never recommend a programme before this is answered.

## Westcliff at a glance

- US private university, Irvine, California. Approved by the California BPPE since 1993. Regionally accredited by **WSCUC**.
- **6 intakes per year.** For the January intake, the application deadline is in **October**.
- **No GMAT, GRE or SAT** required at any level.
- **A 3-year bachelor's degree is accepted** for master's and doctoral entry.
- 9,079 students enrolled (Spring 2026), 103 nationalities, 10:1 student–faculty ratio.
- **Applications do NOT go through the Westcliff website.** Students apply through their consultant. Say this on every Westcliff call.

## EU Business School at a glance

- Founded 1973, Swiss-headquartered private business school. Campuses in Barcelona, Geneva and Munich.
- Member of the OMNES Education Group. 4 QS Stars for excellence in business education.
- Accredited by ACBSP, IACBE, IQA and eduQua.
- Degrees are layered — most programmes award a Spanish *título propio*, an EU Business School Switzerland degree, and a UK or Irish state-recognised degree.
- **Geneva costs roughly twice Barcelona or Munich.** This is the single biggest fact on any EU call — see the tuition section below.

## Bachelor's entry (Westcliff)

- Diploma and academic transcripts
- Minimum undergraduate GPA **2.0**
- English: TOEFL **60** / IELTS **5.5** / Duolingo **95**
- No GMAT, GRE or SAT
- 3-year bachelor's degrees accepted where applicable

## Master's and doctorate entry (Westcliff)

- Prior degree and academic transcripts
- Minimum graduate GPA **2.5**
- **A 3-year bachelor's degree is accepted**
- English: TOEFL **79** / IELTS **6.0** / Duolingo **105**
- No GMAT, GRE or SAT

## Nursing entry (Westcliff, Corona campus)

- Bachelor's in any field, minimum GPA **2.75**
- Nine post-baccalaureate prerequisites: Chemistry, Anatomy & Physiology I, Anatomy & Physiology II, Microbiology, Ethics, Critical Thinking, Sociology, Statistics, Psychology
- ATI TEAS entrance exam, $70 per attempt
- Background check, CPR certification, proof of health insurance, clinical clearance
- English requirements for nursing are **not published** — confirm with the College of Nursing

## Two routes around a missing English test score (Westcliff)

1. **MOI letter** — a letter from the institution that awarded the qualifying degree, confirming the student was taught in English. Accepted only when the student cannot supply a test score.
2. **REAL Pathway** — Westcliff's intensive English programme. Undergraduate applicants clear the requirement at **REAL 300**; graduate applicants must reach **REAL 400**.

### REAL Communications Pathway (Westcliff, Irvine)

Five levels mapped to CEFR, 16 weeks each, 18 hours of live classroom instruction per week on campus at Irvine. Students test in at their actual level rather than starting at the bottom. In the final REAL semester the student begins the degree programme alongside it.

Levels: REAL 98 (A1 Beginner), REAL 100 (A2 Elementary), REAL 200 (B1 Lower Intermediate), REAL 300 (B1+ Intermediate), REAL 400 (B2 Upper Intermediate).

Raise REAL early with any student whose score falls short — it keeps an intake alive rather than pushing them to the next session, though it adds 16 weeks per level and its own cost.

## How Westcliff tuition is actually billed

**The academic year is divided into three semesters**, and annual tuition is split across them. So the per-semester figure is what a student actually pays at a time:

| Level | Per year, F-1 on campus | Per semester (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Bachelor's | $19,128 | **$6,376** |
| Master's | $15,030 | **$5,010** |
| Doctorate | $16,560 | **$5,520** |

**Monthly payment plans are available** — the student divides the *semester invoice* into monthly payments, not the annual figure.

**Actual semester cost varies**, and the university has advised this explicitly. Two things move it:

1. **Course load** — a student taking more or fewer credits in a semester pays accordingly. This is why the doctorate's "per year" figure understates years one and two of the DBA, which carry 21 credits rather than 18.
2. **CPT participation** — a student doing CPT must register for an **additional 1-credit course every semester** they are on it. That is an extra cost on top of the tuition figures above.

The final semester invoice, and any CPT-related requirements, should be confirmed before enrolment. If a student wants their exact number, that goes to Akhil.

Tuition is subject to change at the university's discretion; increases take effect the following semester.

**For doctorates, quote the total, not the annual figure.** Tuition is charged per credit, and the DBA loads 21 credits in years one and two, so those years cost more than the "per year" line suggests. The reliable number is the programme total: $55,200 on campus, $48,000 online.

The 2026–27 international schedule also carries a separate **"professional doctorate"** rate of $975 per credit ($58,500 total) without naming which programmes it covers. If a student asks which rate applies to them, that goes to Akhil.

# PART 6 — WESTCLIFF: CPT AND OPT

This is why STEM designation matters. A STEM-designated programme lets an eligible F-1 graduate extend OPT by 24 months — three years of post-graduation work authorisation in total.

| | When | Hours | Duration | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CPT** full time | During the degree | 40 hrs/week | Maximum 1 year | Beyond one year of full-time CPT the student loses OPT eligibility |
| **CPT** part time | During the degree | 20 hrs/week | No limit | Bachelor's, master's and doctorate all eligible; undergraduates must complete one academic year first |

**Students on CPT must register for an additional 1-credit course each semester they are on it.** That is a real added cost on top of published tuition — factor it in when a student asks what a year will actually cost them.
| **OPT** | After graduation | Full or part time | 1 year | Available once per degree level |
| **OPT** STEM extension | After graduation | Full or part time | +2 years, 3 total | STEM-designated programmes only |

**Never assess whether a specific student is eligible for CPT or OPT, and never estimate visa approval chances.** These are immigration matters. Describe what the university publishes; route the individual case to Akhil.

## Career services (Westcliff)

1:1 coaching, workshops, career fairs, the Interstride F-1-friendly job board, Mitras AI for résumés, personal branding and interview preparation. 109 CPT/OPT partnerships. Named partners include Siemens, California Bank & Trust, Tech Coast Angels and Sony.

# PART 7 — WESTCLIFF DOCTORAL PROGRAMMES

## Westcliff — Doctor of Business Administration (DBA)

- **Length:** 3 years. **Credits:** 60. **Intakes:** 6 per year.
- **Tuition:** $48,000 total online ($800/credit), $55,200 total on campus ($920/credit).
- **Where:** all six campuses plus online. Weekly and professional formats at Irvine; professional only at Santa Monica, San Francisco, Dallas, Orlando and Miami.
- **Award:** Doctor of Business Administration, Westcliff University College of Business.

Three years of structured coursework rather than an open-ended research degree. Delivered in a synchronous hybrid or online format built around working professionals.

**Structure:** Year 1 — 21 credits of core foundation. Year 2 — 21 credits of concentration and research methods. Year 3 — 18 credits of concentration plus Doctoral Dissertation Courses I–IV (9 credits).

**Six concentrations:** Strategic Leadership in the 21st Century; Business Intelligence and Data Analytics (STEM); Information Technology Management (STEM); Cybersecurity (STEM); Applied Computer Science (STEM); Web Development and Design (STEM).

**Entry:** prior degree and transcripts, minimum 2.5 GPA, 3-year bachelor's accepted. TOEFL 79 / IELTS 6.0 / Duolingo 105, or MOI letter, or REAL Pathway. No GMAT or GRE.

**Who this suits:** experienced managers, entrepreneurs, technology professionals, consultants and senior professionals who want doctoral-level study alongside work, particularly in business intelligence, data, IT, cybersecurity or applied computer science.

**Who this does not suit:** someone wanting a traditional research-only PhD with an open-ended timeline, or someone who cannot commit to structured coursework for three years.

No maximum time-to-completion limit is published.

## Westcliff — Stackable DBA

- **Length:** 3 years, same as the standard DBA. **Credits:** 60. **Intakes:** 6 per year.
- **Tuition:** identical to the standard DBA — $48,000 online, $55,200 on campus. No additional time or cost.
- **Where:** Irvine and online only.
- **Award:** Doctor of Business Administration plus two doctoral certificates.

The same DBA, restructured so the student collects two doctoral certificates on the way through. Three levels: Level 1 Foundation, Level 2 Concentration, Level 3 Research and Dissertation, ending in publish-ready research.

**Critical eligibility limit:** available **only to domestic and international students currently residing in the United States**. This is not a route for a student applying from overseas. Confirm residence before discussing it.

**Who this suits:** a student already in the US who wants credentials to show an employer before the dissertation is finished.

## Westcliff — Doctor of Education (EdD)

- **Length:** 4 years. **Credits:** 60. **Intakes:** 6 per year.
- **Tuition:** $48,000 total online, $55,200 total on campus. Per year on campus $16,560 at 18 credits.
- **Where:** Irvine and online only. Weekly format.
- **Award:** Doctor of Education in Leadership, Curriculum, and Instruction, Westcliff University College of Education.

Same 60 credits as the DBA, spread over four years instead of three. Ends in a scholarly written report of original research and an oral defence.

**Structure:** Year 1 — 18 credits core. Year 2 — 18 credits core, concentration and research. Year 3 — 12 credits research. Year 4 — 12 credits dissertation/capstone. The dissertation must be completed by the first semester of year four.

**Two concentrations:** Leadership; Curriculum, Instruction and Assessment.

**Neither concentration is STEM-designated** — no 24-month OPT extension.

**Entry:** as the DBA — 2.5 GPA, 3-year bachelor's accepted, TOEFL 79 / IELTS 6.0 / Duolingo 105.

**Who this suits:** educators, school and college leaders, curriculum specialists.

**Who this does not suit:** a student choosing on time-to-finish (the DBA is a year shorter for the same credits), or an F-1 student who wants the STEM OPT extension.

# PART 12 — EU BUSINESS SCHOOL PROGRAMMES

## EU Business School — English Foundation Program

All three campuses. One term, 10 weeks, 30 ECTS. Intakes October, February, June, August. For students whose English is below the bachelor's threshold. On satisfactory completion the student is eligible to enrol in an EU degree programme provided all other requirements are met. Awards an English Foundation Program certificate from EU Business School Switzerland.

## EU Business School — Business Bridging Program

All three campuses. One semester, 13 weeks, 30 ECTS. Intakes October, February, June, August. An academic bridge rather than a language one — management, economics and business law alongside academic and business English. Successful completion grants automatic admission into year one of the bachelor's programmes. Awards a Business Bridging Program certificate from EU Business School Switzerland.

## EU Business School — Bachelor's, Barcelona

7 semesters, 3 years, 240 ECTS. Intakes October, February, June, August. Taught exclusively in English. Core courses in year one, specialisation subjects in semesters three to five, business management modules through year three. Fast-track available via intensive summer semesters.

**Seven specializations:** BBA Business Administration; BA Digital Communication; BA Leisure & Tourism Management; BA International Relations; BA Sports Management; BA Digital Business, Design & Innovation; BA Artificial Intelligence for Business.

**Awards:** university bachelor's degree (título propio) from Universitat de Vic – Universitat Central de Catalunya (UVic-UCC), Spain; ACBSP- and IACBE-accredited bachelor's degree from EU Business School Switzerland; state-recognised BA (Hons) in Business Management from University of Derby, UK.

Optional semester 8 earns a Diploma of Advanced Studies (DAS) in the chosen specialisation. Intercampus transfer, or a one-semester study abroad at an OMNES Education Group sister school, is available.

**Note:** the awarding body changed between brochure editions. The 2024 Barcelona brochure names Universidad Católica San Antonio de Murcia (UCAM) for the título propio; the current edition names UVic-UCC. Confirm which applies to the student's intake year.

## EU Business School — BA (Hons) in Business Management, Barcelona

6 semesters, 3 years, 180 ECTS. Intakes October and February. A standalone UK honours route. Year one is core business; years two and three fold in the chosen pathway, closing with a business consultancy project.

**Four pathways:** International Business; Marketing; Finance; Enterprise.

**Award:** state-recognised BA (Hons) in Business Management in the chosen pathway from University of Derby, UK. Additionally accredited by the Chartered Management Institute (CMI) — students can earn CMI's Diploma in Management and Leadership as an extra international qualification.

## EU Business School — Bachelor's, Geneva

6 or 7 semesters, 3 years, 210 or 240 ECTS. Intakes October, February, June, August. Option 1 is six semesters and 210 ECTS. Option 2 runs seven semesters and 240 ECTS and adds the University of Derby honours degree.

**Geneva requires an internship and internship report to graduate** — Barcelona and Munich do not.

**Seven specializations:** BBA Business Administration; BA Leisure & Tourism Management; BA International Relations; BA Sports Management; BA Digital Business, Design & Innovation; BA Artificial Intelligence for Business; BS Business Finance.

**Awards:** ACBSP- and IACBE-accredited bachelor's degree from EU Business School Switzerland; título propio from UVic-UCC, Spain; Option 2 only, state-recognised BA (Hons) in Business Management from University of Derby.

No Digital Communication specialisation at Geneva. Business Finance is Geneva and Munich only.

## EU Business School — Bachelor's, Munich

7 semesters, 3 years, 240 ECTS. Munich pairs every bachelor's specialisation with the Irish honours degree — the two are awarded together. Specialisation subjects in semesters three to five, business management in year three, closing with a capstone and two final cases presented to a panel.

**Eight majors:** BBA Business Administration; BA Digital Communication; BA Leisure & Tourism Management; BA International Relations; BA Sports Management; BA Digital Business, Design & Innovation; BA Artificial Intelligence for Business; BS Business Finance.

**Awards:** título propio from UVic-UCC; Certificate of Specialization from EU Business School Switzerland; state-recognised Dublin Business School BA (Hons) in Business via Quality and Qualifications Ireland (QQI), EQF level 6 / NFQ level 8.

Delivered under Article 112 of the Bavarian Higher Education Innovation Act (BayHIG).

**Note:** the Munich brochure is internally inconsistent on intakes — body text says October, February, June, August; the summary panel says October, January, June, August. Verify the February/January start with admissions.

## EU Business School — BA (Hons) in Business, Munich

6 semesters, 3 years, 180 ECTS. Intakes October and February. The Irish honours degree taken on its own. Common core in year one; electives in years two and three.

**Six pathways:** BA (Hons) in Business; Business (Management); Business (Project Management); Business (HR Management); Business (Law); Business (Information Systems).

**Award:** state-recognised Dublin Business School BA (Hons) in Business via QQI, EQF level 6 / NFQ level 8.

## EU Business School — Master's, Barcelona

3 terms, 1 year full time, 60 ECTS. Intakes October, January, March. Each term combines subject courses with a project-based module worked in transversal groups, plus a skills-development module. Graduation requires a Final Capstone Project.

**Ten master's:** Management; Marketing; Finance; Tourism & Hospitality Management; Innovation & Entrepreneurship; Digital Business; Fashion & Luxury Business; Digital Marketing, Transformation & Design Thinking; Business Analytics & Data Science; Artificial Intelligence for Business.

**Awards:** university master's degree (título propio) from UVic-UCC; master's degree from EU Business School Switzerland.

**Add-on:** MSc International Business Management from London Metropolitan University, UK — requires an additional 14-week Business Consultancy Project module (30 ECTS) plus a Research Module.

## EU Business School — Master's, Geneva

3 terms, 1 year full time, 60 ECTS. Intakes October, January, March. **Geneva substitutes an internship and internship report for the capstone** as the graduation requirement.

**Eight master's:** Management; Finance; Innovation & Entrepreneurship; Digital Business; Fashion & Luxury Business; Digital Marketing, Transformation & Design Thinking; Business Analytics & Data Science; Artificial Intelligence for Business. Marketing and Tourism & Hospitality Management are not offered at Geneva.

**Awards:** master's degree from EU Business School Switzerland; título propio from UVic-UCC, Spain. Add-on: London Metropolitan University MSc International Business Management.

## EU Business School — Master in Digital International Relations and Diplomacy, Geneva

3 terms, 1 year full time, 60 ECTS. Intakes October, January, March. **Geneva only, and the single joint-degree programme in the set.** Built with UNITAR, the UN Institute for Training and Research, for students aiming at UN organisations. Each term runs two subject courses plus a project module and a skills module, with access to real conferences and negotiations.

**Award:** joint master's degree from UNITAR and EU Business School Switzerland.

Internship and internship report required to graduate. EU Business School states it is the only institution in Geneva with direct affiliations of this kind.

## EU Business School — Master's, Munich

3 terms, 1 year full time, 60 ECTS. Intakes October, January, March. The same ten specialisations as Barcelona, with a Final Capstone Project to graduate.

**Awards:** título propio from UVic-UCC, Spain; Certificate in Advanced Studies from EU Business School Switzerland. Munich awards a Certificate in Advanced Studies rather than a second full master's degree.

Delivered under Article 112 of the BayHIG. Add-on: London Metropolitan University MSc International Business Management.

## EU Business School — MBA, Barcelona

3 terms, 1 year full time, 90 ECTS. Intakes October, January, March. A single programme with no majors — the differentiator is that the degree itself is UK state-recognised. Rolling intake: students can start at the beginning of any of the three terms, then move straight into the capstone.

**Structure:** 20 ECTS per term across three terms, then a 30-ECTS capstone. Core courses include Business Problem Solving, Breakthrough Leadership Skills, Financial Decision Making and Digital Business Delivery.

**Awards:** UK state-recognised degree from London Metropolitan University; degree from EU Business School Switzerland.

**Entry:** minimum 2:2 Honours degree or equivalent; IELTS 6.5 with at least 5.5 per section / TOEFL 93 iBT; at least two years' work experience expected, which may have been gained while studying; selection interview may be required.

## EU Business School — MSc International Business Management, Barcelona

3 terms, 1 year full time, 90 ECTS. Intakes October, January, March. The pre-experience counterpart to the Barcelona MBA, for students without two years of management experience. Same rolling intake and same 20 + 20 + 20 + 30 ECTS capstone structure.

Core courses include Operations and Technology Management, International Business Strategy with Simulation, and Sustainability in Global Companies.

**Awards:** UK state-recognised degree from London Metropolitan University; degree from EU Business School Switzerland.

## EU Business School — MBA, Geneva

3 terms 1 year full time, or 6 terms 2 years part time. 90 ECTS. Intakes October, January, March. The widest major choice of any EU MBA. Full-time students should expect 30 to 40 hours a week. Graduation requires an internship and internship report, supported by a mandatory Research Methods module and internship seminars.

**Six majors:** International Business; International Marketing; Global Banking & Finance; Leisure & Tourism Management; Digital Business; Sports Management. Leisure & Tourism Management is a Geneva-only MBA major.

**Awards:** ACBSP- and IACBE-accredited MBA from EU Business School Switzerland; university MBA degree (título propio) from UVic-UCC, Spain.

**Second qualification available:** a London Metropolitan University degree — MSc International Business Management (extra business consultancy project plus business competency simulation) or MBA (extra building business insights report plus business competency simulation). The London Met MSc suits under two years' experience; the London Met MBA suits two or more years in managerial roles.

## EU Business School — MBA, Munich

3 terms 1 year full time, or 6 terms 2 years part time. 90 ECTS. Intakes October, January, March. Five majors, culminating in a dissertation rather than an internship report.

**Five majors:** International Business; International Marketing; Global Banking & Finance; Digital Business; Sports Management. No Leisure & Tourism Management at Munich.

**Awards:** título propio MBA from UVic-UCC, Spain; Certificate of Advanced Studies from EU Business School Switzerland.

**Second qualification:** London Metropolitan University MSc International Business Management or MBA, studied in a hybrid learning environment.

Delivered under Article 112 of the BayHIG.
